THE BOOK

What did the last decade really cost the middle class?

The economic bargain changed. Inflation eroded purchasing power. Debt expanded. Housing moved further out of reach. The book measures those changes not only in dollars, but in productive life and economic freedom.

Money is stored time. That is the central idea connecting the book, the calculator and the broader project.
